How it works

The rules as code

The official 1v1 Might rules: movement and zone of control, 25 techs, every unit from Warrior to Giant, ruins, roads, monuments, and the Drylands map generator.

What the network sees

One player's view as 60 planes (terrain, resources, buildings, borders, cities, units and their health) plus 45 numbers for stars, income, turn, techs and score. On an 11×11 map there are 30,641 possible actions; illegal ones are masked before the network picks.

How it learned

A population of agents with different reward styles trained in parallel; each generation the weakest were replaced and a champion promoted, while rush and economy exploiters hunted for weaknesses.

How Legion2 decides

It runs the Alpha1 network, and whenever the network is less than 95% sure, it takes its four favorite moves, plays each forward four ways, and keeps the network's pick unless another move is clearly better. That search is worth +159 Elo.
